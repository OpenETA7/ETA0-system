# Articulated Interaction Guidance (Drawers, Doors, Knobs)

- Inspect the handle or knob geometry in both views to identify the pull or push axis before making contact.
- Approach perpendicular to the handle face with the gripper oriented to straddle the handle cleanly, then close the fingers around it.
- Pull or push smoothly along the drawer slide or door arc in moderate segments rather than a single oversized jump.
- Verify from the fresh observation that the drawer or door has actually reached the target open or closed state before moving on or reporting `finish`.
