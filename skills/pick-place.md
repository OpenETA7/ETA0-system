# Pick and Place Guidance

- Confirm object identity visually across both external and wrist views before descending.
- Align horizontally over the object center before lowering to grasp height; avoid sweeping laterally through cluttered neighbors.
- After closing the gripper, lift slightly and verify object lift in the fresh observation before transporting toward the destination.
- Position directly above the target container or surface before releasing; confirm the object rests stably inside or on the destination before reporting `finish`.
